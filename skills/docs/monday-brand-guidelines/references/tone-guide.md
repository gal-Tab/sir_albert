# Monday.com Tone & Voice Guide

Comprehensive examples showing the Monday.com voice in action across different contexts, with before/after corrections.

## Core Voice Principles

Monday.com's voice is:
- **Confident**: Expert without arrogance
- **Fluff-free**: Direct, no jargon or buzzwords
- **Playful**: Light touches, never silly
- **Passionate**: Genuine enthusiasm for customer success

## Voice Spectrum

**Dial up confidence** when: Positioning against competitors, showcasing expertise, enterprise contexts
**Dial up playfulness** when: Celebrating wins, onboarding, community content
**Dial up directness** when: UI copy, errors, help documentation
**Dial up passion** when: Customer stories, mission-driven content

## Common Mistakes & Corrections

### Mistake: Overusing "seamlessly"

❌ "Seamlessly integrate your favorite tools"
✅ "Connect your favorite tools"

❌ "Seamlessly collaborate with your team"
✅ "Work together in real time"

### Mistake: Excessive enthusiasm

❌ "We're SO excited to announce this AMAZING new feature!!!"
✅ "New: Automation builder for custom workflows"

❌ "This is absolutely incredible!"
✅ "This changes everything" (if truly significant)

### Mistake: Corporate jargon

❌ "Leverage our platform to drive synergies"
✅ "Use monday.com to keep teams aligned"

❌ "Optimize your operational efficiency"
✅ "Save time and work smarter"

### Mistake: Vague benefits

❌ "Improve your productivity"
✅ "Save 5 hours per week on status updates"

❌ "Better collaboration"
✅ "Everyone sees what's happening, no meetings needed"

### Mistake: Passive voice

❌ "Workflows can be customized to fit your needs"
✅ "Customize workflows to fit your needs"

❌ "Updates are automatically sent when tasks are completed"
✅ "Get automatic updates when tasks are done"

### Mistake: Title Case

❌ "Get Started With Your Free Trial Today"
✅ "Get started with your free trial today"

❌ "Create Your First Workflow"
✅ "Create your first workflow"

### Mistake: Periods on headlines

❌ "Work without limits."
✅ "Work without limits"

❌ "See your entire project at a glance."
✅ "See your entire project at a glance"

## Power Words Aligned with Monday Voice

### Use these confidently:

- **Action**: Build, create, track, see, run, ship, manage
- **Benefits**: Save time, get organized, stay aligned, move faster
- **Simplicity**: Simple, easy, clear, straightforward
- **Outcomes**: Done, finished, complete, ready, shipped
- **Scale**: Grow, scale, expand, unlimited
- **Control**: Own, manage, customize, control

### Avoid these (jargon/overused):

- Leverage, utilize, facilitate, enable
- Seamlessly, effortlessly (unless genuinely true)
- Revolutionary, game-changing, transformative
- Synergy, paradigm, ecosystem
- Robust, comprehensive, holistic
- Next-generation, cutting-edge, innovative (as adjectives)

## Tone Testing

When reviewing Monday.com copy, ask:

1. **Confident?**: Does it sound expert without arrogant?
2. **Fluff-free?**: Could we cut any words and maintain meaning?
3. **Playful?**: Is there a light touch, or is it too serious/silly?
4. **Passionate?**: Does it show we care about customer success?
5. **Active voice?**: Are we using verbs, not passive constructions?
6. **Jargon-free?**: Would a non-expert understand immediately?
7. **Sentence case?**: No Title Case or CAPS?
8. **Specific?**: Are benefits concrete, not vague?

If you answer "no" to any of these, revise.

## Final Checklist

Before approving Monday.com copy:

- [ ] No jargon from the blacklist
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] Sentence case (no Title Case)
- [ ] No periods on headlines
- [ ] Specific benefits (not vague)
- [ ] Short sentences (15 words max preferred)
- [ ] Conversational but professional
- [ ] Speaks to customer pain points
- [ ] Clear call to action (when applicable)
- [ ] Product voice aligned (premium/spicy/edgy/dynamic)

## Quick Reference

**When stuck, default to**:
- Active voice: "Create workflows" not "Workflows can be created"
- Simple words: "Use" not "Leverage"
- Short sentences: 10-15 words average
- Concrete benefits: "Save 5 hours" not "Improve efficiency"
- Conversational: Write like you speak
- Optimistic: Focus on positive outcomes

The Monday.com voice is confident, clear, and human. Avoid corporate speak, trust your reader's intelligence, and focus on helping them succeed.
